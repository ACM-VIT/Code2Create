# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Code2Create participants and team leads use the portal before and during a 48-hour event. They need to finish registration, form and manage a team, write or revise a proposal, understand admission and review state, find operational information, and get help without learning the event's internal systems.

## Product Purpose

The participant portal is the trusted, realtime place for each participant's current event state and next legal action. It should make the next task obvious, keep team and application status easy to reach, and prevent navigation or presentation from obscuring deadlines, permissions, or accepted backend state.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, warm, and assured. The portal's night-hanami scene, botanical avatars, restrained rose light, and plainspoken microcopy should make a high-pressure event feel calm and personal without turning routine workflows into spectacle.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard chrome that fights the illustrated scene, generic SaaS card grids, dense admin navigation, and clever menus that hide unrelated destinations inside team or account details. In particular, do not combine roster inspection, team management, global navigation, and sign-out in one undifferentiated popover.

## Design Principles

- Put the participant's next action and current state ahead of secondary destinations.
- Give global navigation, team context, and account actions distinct, predictable homes.
- Preserve the portal's calm botanical character while using familiar product affordances.
- Reveal capability-dependent destinations without making the overall structure move unpredictably.
- Use concise, human language that tells participants where they are and what happens next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain full keyboard access, visible focus, semantic navigation and menu labeling, screen-reader names, adequate contrast in both night and daybreak themes, touch targets suitable for phones, and reduced-motion behavior. No formal WCAG conformance level is claimed by the repository, so implementation should improve these fundamentals without inventing a certification target.
