# Reviewer flow

The reviewer app teaches the flow through the work itself. There is no training status, tutorial to complete or ops sign-off. Ops controls eligibility with the reviewer’s active profile, skills and round assignment.

1. **Confirm availability.** Choose the whole round or your hours when the confirmation window opens.
2. **Set up the phone.** The app shows camera, review alerts and offline-score readiness. The round decides which permissions are required.
3. **Clock in.** Join your assigned panel or wait as a reserve. Clock-in checks confirmed hours and phone readiness.
4. **Meet the team.** Scan its review QR, or type the code beneath it. Report any conflict before starting. The coordinator starts once everyone is present.
5. **Review.** Each step shows one fixed question, its 0–4 scores and the selected score’s anchor. Scores 0 and 4 require a reason. Suggested questions are optional and ungraded. Project details open separately.
6. **Finish scores.** Discussion ends on the timer or when the coordinator ends it. Each reviewer checks their scores and leaves their own final comment before submitting. Drafts save automatically and recover after reopening the app.
7. **Complete the review.** Once every scorecard is submitted and required panel notes are saved, finish the review to free the panel for the next team.

Between reviews, reviewers can take a break or end their shift. A substitute request offers Accept or Decline, then arrival and clock-in. Duty cannot end during an active review or unfinished scoring. GPS asks for confirmation; it does not clock someone out by itself.

Submitted scores require an ops-approved correction to change. Help appears only for an unavailable action, failed request or decision with a consequence.
