Participant policy

Last updated August 31, 2026

These are the rules for taking part. They are short on purpose. By registering, you agree to them.

Participation

Eligibility, registration windows, and team rules are announced for each edition. Register with accurate information about yourself. Registering with someone else's identity, or misrepresenting your eligibility, ends your participation.

Accounts

One account per person, and it has to be yours. Keep access to your account to yourself; you are responsible for what happens through it.

Original work

What you enter is your team's own work, created during the event. Public libraries, frameworks, and tools are fine to use. Presenting pre-built work, or work that is not yours, as your entry is not.

Conduct

Be someone people want on a team. Harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, and sabotage have no place here, in person or online. Follow the instructions of organizers and volunteers.

If something feels wrong, tell us early. Reach the organizers through the event's support channels.

Venue and safety

Carry a valid ID and follow the venue's rules on access, timings, and safety. Instructions from venue staff and organizers apply to everyone.

Your work

Your team owns what it builds, full stop. Events are photographed and recorded, and coverage may include your team name, project name, and demo.

Removal

Organizers may remove a participant or team that breaks this policy. These calls are made by the organizing team and are final.

Your data

What we collect and why lives in the privacy notice. The short version: we collect what registration asks for, we use it to run the event, and we do not sell it.

Changes

This policy may be updated between editions. The version on this page is the one that applies.

Questions

Reach ACM-VIT at acmvit.in.
